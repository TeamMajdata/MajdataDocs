# Skins

Download public skins from [GitHub](https://github.com/TeamMajdata/MajdataPlay-Skins) or [Tencent CNB](https://cnb.cool/TeamMajdata/MajdataPlay-Skins).

Place each skin in the `Skins` folder next to the [configuration file](/en/majdataplay/configuration/). Follow the structure of the default skin:

```text
Skins/
│
├── Light2/
│   ├── outline.png
│   ├── SubBackgourd.png
│   └── ...
│
├── Skin name/
│   ├── outline.png
│   └── ...
│
└── Skin name/
    ├── outline.png
    └── ...
```
