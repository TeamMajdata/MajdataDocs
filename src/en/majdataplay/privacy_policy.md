# Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to MajdataPlay. MajdataPlay is an open-source project, and its source code is publicly available. This Privacy Policy explains how the game collects, uses, and protects your information.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 No Personally Identifiable Information

MajdataPlay does not require registration and does not actively collect your name, address, email address, phone number, or other personally identifiable information.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data security. However, no method of transmission over the Internet can guarantee absolute security.

## 4. Local Data Storage

The game may store the following data locally on your device:

- Game settings
- Game levels
- Chart scores
- Game logs

This data remains on your device and is not uploaded automatically.

## 5. Open-Source Notice

MajdataPlay is an open-source project. You can review the source code to understand how data is handled. Community review and contributions are welcome.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on the project page.

## 7. Contact Information

If you have questions about privacy, contact us through:

- The project homepage
- The repository issue tracker
- Email at <admin@majdata.net>
