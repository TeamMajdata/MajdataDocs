# FAQ

This page answers common questions about MajdataPlay.

## Why can't OBS record MajdataPlay audio? Why does MajdataPlay stop audio or video from other apps?

Turn off exclusive audio mode for MajdataPlay in the settings.

## How do I move the game window to another display?

Press `Alt` + `Enter` to leave full-screen mode. Drag the window to the other display, then press `Alt` + `Enter` again to return to full-screen mode.
