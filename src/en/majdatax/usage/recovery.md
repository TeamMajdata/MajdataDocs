# Recover an autosave

## Open the autosave recovery panel

Open the autosave recovery panel from the **Start Page** or under **File > Autosave Recovery**.

![Autosave recovery panel](/majdatax/usage/recovery/panel.png)

Select the autosave you want to restore in the top-left corner. Use the bottom-left controls to jump to the corresponding difficulty line. After checking the content, click **Recover**. You can also click **Open** to open the corresponding chart file directly. If you recover the currently open chart, MajdataX reloads it automatically.

**Recovery replaces the entire file. The difficulty navigation only helps you inspect its contents.**
