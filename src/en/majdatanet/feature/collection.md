## Get started

You can create your own playlist or play someone else's public playlist. <mark>You must subscribe to a playlist before it appears in the game.</mark>

1. Open [MajdataNet](https://majdata.net), register an account, and sign in.
2. To create a playlist:
   1. Open the page for a song you want to add.
   2. Click **Favorite** on the left.
   3. Click **New Collection**.
   4. Enter a **Name** and choose its visibility.

      ::: tip
      Choose **Public** if you want other people to play it. Choose **Private** if it is only for you.
      :::

   5. Click **Create**.
   6. After the collection is created, click it to add the current song.
3. To play someone else's public playlist:
   1. Open **Playlist Plaza** from the navigation bar.
   2. Click the heart in the top-right corner of a playlist card to subscribe.
4. Open **My Collections** from the account menu in the navigation bar.
5. Playlists listed under **Subscribed Playlists** will appear in the game.
